Behind the lens

Stefanos Papadakis

Not a photographer. Just someone who stops walking when the light does something it shouldn’t.

By day he lives among numbers and other people’s futures. The camera arrived later, almost by accident, as a way of keeping what words refused to hold.

He won’t tell you where every picture was taken, or who is in them. Half of each story stays with him. The other half is yours now.

Every face here is only half.
You carry the other half.
